# Friend-group travel apps (August 2026)

## Planning apps

Free incumbents everywhere; every per-seat Pro is stuck at indie scale.

- **Wanderlog**: free core, Pro ~$40/yr; ~1.5M monthly users, ~$100k/month revenue after 7 years. Nothing for "one pays, group gets Pro".
- **TripIt** (SAP Concur): Pro $49/yr, a confirmation inbox, not a group tool. Sabre's **TripCase shut 1 Apr 2025**: aggregation alone does not sustain a product.
- **Troupe**: built in-house by JetBlue as a funnel; polls and voting, no money.
- **Polarsteps**: the one winner (10M to 18M+ users in a year, ~$10M revenue). **No subscription**; money from printed books plus affiliate.
- **Airbnb**: group wishlists and messaging (>80% of bookings are group trips), shared itinerary in 2026. The incumbent most likely to absorb group planning.
- **Google Maps lists / Notion / Sheets**: the default; "that one friend with the spreadsheet".
- A long tail of 2026 "alternatives" signals low barriers and no differentiation.

## Expense apps

- **Splitwise**: Pro $4.99/mo; free tier capped ~3 expenses/day; one Pro member does not unblock the group; no UPI, the top complaint in India (price went ₹999 to ₹2,499/yr). 10M+ MAU, profitable, users "leaving in droves".
- **Tricount** (bunq): fully free, an acquisition channel; 17M+ users, marketing to Splitwise refugees in India.
- **Settle Up**: per-trip pricing, one payer upgrades the group (1 week $5.99 to year $39.99). The cleanest per-trip model.
- **Splid**: no account, offline; loved in Europe.
- **Indian UPI-linked** apps (Niptao, FairShare, Splitkaro...): "UPI integration is a must-have".
- **Paytm Split Bills**: free, unlimited, launched 29 Jul 2026.

Common complaints: caps, ads, sign-up friction, everyone must install, no payment rail, ambiguous conversion rates.

## Why WhatsApp + Sheets + Splitwise still wins

- **Install asymmetry**: one planner is motivated, 5–10 others are not; every dedicated app taxes onboarding per member per trip.
- **Trips are episodic**: a per-seat annual sub is the wrong shape for twice a year.
- **Sharing bolted on** to single-player tools.
- **Money needs a rail, not a ledger**: settlement happens on UPI/Venmo/cash anyway.
- **Decisions happen in chat** and get buried.

## What groups complain about

Experian (June 2025, n>700 US adults): only **1 in 4** groups set a budget; **>50%** of Gen Z/millennials had a money disagreement on a friends' trip; **1 in 5 ended a friendship** over it. Credit Karma (July 2025): 38% took on debt for wedding/bachelor trips; 56% feel obligated to go.

Themes: the planner's resentment, scope creep, freeloading, chat silence ("let me check", "maybe April?"), mismatched pace. Complaints cluster on **money fairness and awkwardness**, **decision paralysis and flakiness**, and **one person carrying the load**, not on missing itinerary features.

## Market

- US: 24% of travellers expect to travel with friends in 2025.
- **India outbound: record 32.71M departures in 2025 (+5.9%)**, ~50M by 2030.
- Indian arrivals 2025: **Thailand 2.49M (+16.8%)** while total Thai arrivals fell 7.2%; **Vietnam ~746k (+48.9%)**; **Sri Lanka 531k (+27.5%)**; **Bali ~569k**; UAE 3.5M (2024); Japan 315k (+35%).
- Visa-free or on-arrival for Indians now covers Thailand, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives, Mauritius, Philippines, Indonesia (VoA), Vietnam (e-visa).

The Indian friend group going to SE Asia is the fastest-growing, most price-sensitive, most WhatsApp/UPI-native segment, and no app above is built for it.

## Monetisation that worked

Per-trip, one-pays-for-all (Settle Up); loss-leader for a financial product (Tricount, Airbnb, JetBlue); keepsake plus affiliate with no subscription (Polarsteps). Freemium with a cap burned Splitwise's brand; per-seat annual Pro fails for groups; B2B2C and booking take-rate need volume group apps lack.

## Implications

- Do not build another itinerary app, nor a pure split app (crowded, Paytm is free) unless UPI-native.
- The unsolved pain is social: a *mechanism that changes behaviour inside the group* (commitment, visible accountability, a game) that a spreadsheet cannot do.
- Ship inside WhatsApp: link-joinable, no install, one owner, guests see the page before joining.
- Price per trip to one person, or take nothing from the group and monetise a keepsake/affiliate layer.
- Beachhead: Indian outbound SE Asia groups.
- Beware absorption by Airbnb, Google Maps and WhatsApp; the defensible part is the social mechanic and the group's own history.

Sources: Experian 2025; Credit Karma/Fortune; India MoT tourism snapshot 2025; Splitwise, Settle Up, Polarsteps, Wanderlog, TripCase, Troupe and Paytm press and listings.
